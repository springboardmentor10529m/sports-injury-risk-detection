# Glossary

* **ROM (Range of Motion)**: The full movement potential of a joint.
* **RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion)**: Subjective measure of exercise intensity.
* **ACL (Anterior Cruciate Ligament)**: A key knee ligament, prone to injury.
* **Biomechanical deviation**: Movement diverging from optimal or safe mechanics.
* **Movement asymmetry**: Imbalance in mechanics between left and right sides.
* **Composite risk score**: Weighted aggregation of various risk factors.
* **Keypoint / joint landmark**: Identified coordinates of body joints in video frames.
* **Pose estimation**: CV task of finding keypoints.
* **Decision-support signal**: System outputs designed to aid professionals, not to serve as a definitive diagnosis.
* **Training load**: Cumulative physical stress on an athlete.
* **Fatigue indicator**: Markers suggesting physical exhaustion.
* **Overuse injury**: Injury resulting from repetitive micro-trauma.
* **Transfer learning**: Using a pre-trained model for a new but related task.
* **Autoencoder**: Neural network architecture used here for anomaly detection.
